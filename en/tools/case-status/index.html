Case Status & POA Lookup

The right official channels for checking a case status or verifying a power of attorney, plus firm support if you need it.

Checking a case status or verifying a power of attorney requires accessing the Ministry of Justice's official Najiz platform directly, which this page guides you to rather than simulating. Message us on WhatsApp if you'd like help accessing or interpreting the result.

This page is part of an independent lawyer directory. It gives general information on legal services in Saudi Arabia, which is not legal advice, and the directory is not a law firm.

Checking Case Status

Case status for a filed matter can be checked directly through your account on the Ministry of Justice's Najiz electronic judiciary platform, which shows case status, upcoming hearing dates, and any notifications.

Verifying a Power of Attorney

A power of attorney issued in Saudi Arabia can be verified through the Ministry of Justice's electronic verification services, which confirm the document is registered and still valid.

Why there's no direct search box here

Accessing real case and POA data requires logging into the Ministry of Justice's official government systems, which this firm's website cannot directly access or simulate. Rather than showing a search tool that couldn't actually retrieve your data, the lawyers point you to the correct official channel, and the lawyers can also follow up on your behalf.

  • To check a case: log into your account at Najiz (najiz.sa)
  • To verify a POA: use the Ministry of Justice's electronic verification service
  • If you have trouble accessing or interpreting the result, message us and the lawyers will help
