Direct Answers

Is this calculator legally binding or official?

No — it's an educational estimate for common family structures. Actual estate distribution should be confirmed by a qualified attorney, particularly for anything beyond a straightforward spouse/children/parents structure.

What if my family includes siblings or grandparents of the deceased?

This calculator doesn't model those relationships, since they introduce additional rules beyond its current scope. Message the firm on WhatsApp and we'll calculate your specific case precisely.
