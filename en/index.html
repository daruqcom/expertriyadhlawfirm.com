Common Questions

What does a MISA license allow a foreign company to do in Riyadh?

A MISA licence permits a foreign-owned entity to operate legally in Saudi Arabia, including full or majority ownership in most sectors. Independent lawyers in the directory can assist with the application, structuring and post-licensing compliance.

Can a lawyer from the directory represent a company before Riyadh's commercial courts?

Some independent lawyers in the directory practise before Riyadh's commercial courts and file through the Najiz electronic judiciary platform. Confirm the lawyer's licence and scope of work with them directly before engaging.

What happens when I message the directory?
