Direct Answers

What does a MISA license allow a foreign company to do in Riyadh?

A MISA license permits a foreign-owned entity to operate legally in Saudi Arabia, including full or majority ownership in most sectors. The firm handles the application, structuring, and post-licensing compliance from Riyadh.

Can this firm represent a company before Riyadh's commercial courts?

Yes — the litigation team represents clients before Riyadh's commercial courts and files through the Najiz electronic judiciary platform, covering the full lifecycle from filing to enforcement.

What happens when I message the firm?

A licensed attorney replies directly — usually the same day — to understand your matter and outline how the firm can help. Fees are confirmed upfront before any engagement begins.
