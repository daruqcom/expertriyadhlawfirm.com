Energy & Resources — Sector Legal Services

Energy and resources businesses in Riyadh need counsel spanning sector-specific regulatory compliance, joint venture structuring, and the contract complexity typical of major energy projects. Contact the firm on WhatsApp to discuss the specifics of your case.

A sector tied to national strategy

This sector connects closely with Saudi Arabia's major national energy initiatives, and legal work here often spans both general corporate structuring and sector-specific regulatory questions at once, reflecting how deeply energy policy is woven into the Kingdom's broader economic direction.

Businesses operating in this space, whether established players or newer entrants exploring renewable or diversified energy projects, typically need counsel comfortable moving between corporate transactional work and the specific regulatory frameworks that govern energy activity specifically.

Structuring joint ventures for major projects

Energy projects at scale frequently involve joint ventures between multiple parties — a foreign technical partner, a Saudi entity, sometimes a government-adjacent stakeholder — and structuring these relationships properly from the outset determines how smoothly the venture actually operates once underway.

Our Corporate & Commercial practice handles this structuring work directly, addressing governance, profit distribution, and exit mechanisms with the same rigor whether the underlying project is a traditional energy facility or an emerging renewable initiative.

Regulatory compliance specific to the energy sector

Beyond general corporate structure, energy businesses face regulatory requirements specific to the sector — licensing, environmental compliance tied to energy production, and coordination with the relevant national authorities overseeing energy activity in the Kingdom.

Our Sector-Specific Law practice covers these requirements directly, and we find that businesses new to the Saudi energy sector particularly benefit from understanding this regulatory landscape before committing significant capital to a project.

Mergers, acquisitions, and consolidation in the sector

As Saudi Arabia's energy sector continues to develop, consolidation activity — acquisitions, mergers between existing players, and strategic partnerships — has become a regular feature of the landscape, each requiring careful due diligence given the scale and long-term nature of energy assets.

We support both the buy-side and sell-side of these transactions, with particular attention to environmental liabilities and regulatory approvals that can meaningfully affect a deal's actual value and timeline.

Contracts that reflect genuine project complexity

Major energy projects involve contracts considerably more complex than standard commercial agreements — long-term supply arrangements, technical partnership terms, and risk allocation provisions that need to account for genuinely long project timelines spanning years or even decades.

We draft and review these agreements with attention to how they'll actually perform over that extended timeline, not just how they read at the moment of signing.

Working alongside government-adjacent entities

Given the sector's national importance, energy businesses frequently interact with government or semi-government entities in some capacity, whether through licensing, project partnerships, or regulatory oversight, and this interaction often benefits from attorneys familiar with both commercial and administrative law dimensions.

We coordinate across our Corporate & Commercial and Public & Regulatory practices where a matter genuinely spans both, rather than requiring a client to separately engage different specialists for what is, in practice, one connected situation.
