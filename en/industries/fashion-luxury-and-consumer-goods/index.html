Fashion, Luxury & Consumer Goods — Sector Legal Services

Fashion, luxury, and consumer goods businesses need counsel on trademark protection, anti-counterfeiting enforcement, and consumer protection compliance. Message the firm on WhatsApp to discuss your brand.

A sector where brand value is the core asset

This sector's value is closely tied to brand protection, making trademark registration and counterfeiting enforcement especially central to the legal work involved, since a fashion or luxury brand's entire commercial value often rests on the strength and exclusivity of its brand identity.

This makes IP protection considerably more central here than in many other commercial sectors, where brand identity plays a supporting rather than primary commercial role.

Trademark registration for fashion and luxury brands

This falls under our Trademark Registration service within IP, and registering a brand name, logo, and often distinctive design elements early provides the legal foundation for everything else — enforcement against counterfeiters, licensing arrangements, and brand expansion all depend on this registration being properly secured first.

We help brands understand the full scope of what's protectable, since fashion and luxury brands often have more protectable elements than founders initially realize — distinctive patterns, packaging, and even specific color combinations can sometimes receive protection.

Anti-counterfeiting enforcement

This connects to our Anti-Counterfeiting service, also within IP, and counterfeit goods represent a genuine commercial threat to fashion and luxury brands specifically, both through direct lost sales and through the brand dilution that comes from counterfeit products bearing a brand's name.

We help brands pursue enforcement action against counterfeiters, understanding that effective enforcement often requires coordinated action across multiple fronts — online marketplaces, physical retail, and customs enforcement at points of import.

Consumer protection for fashion and luxury retail

Fashion and luxury retailers face consumer protection considerations around product authenticity guarantees, return policies for high-value items, and accurate representation of materials and craftsmanship, areas where consumer expectations run particularly high given the price points involved.

We help brands structure these consumer-facing policies to build genuine trust while protecting the business from disputes.

Licensing and brand extension agreements

As fashion and luxury brands extend into new product categories or geographic markets through licensing arrangements, structuring these agreements requires careful attention to quality control provisions that protect the core brand from dilution through poorly executed licensed products.

We help brands structure licensing terms that enable genuine expansion while maintaining the quality standards that justify the brand's premium positioning in the first place.

E-commerce and authentication in digital retail

Fashion and luxury e-commerce raises specific authentication challenges, since online sales channels are particularly vulnerable to counterfeit infiltration, and we help brands structure verification systems and marketplace enforcement strategies specific to digital retail channels.

This digital dimension has become considerably more important as fashion and luxury commerce has moved increasingly online.

Disputes in fashion and luxury brand relationships

Where a dispute arises — a counterfeiting matter, a licensing disagreement, a consumer protection complaint — we handle these with genuine understanding of how brand value actually works in this sector, coordinating with our Disputes & Litigation practice where formal proceedings become necessary.

This sector-specific understanding meaningfully strengthens how these disputes are actually resolved, since brand reputation considerations often matter alongside the immediate legal question.
