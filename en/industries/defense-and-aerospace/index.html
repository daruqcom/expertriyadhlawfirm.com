Defense & Aerospace — Sector Legal Services

Defense and aerospace businesses need counsel on government contracting, aviation-specific regulatory matters, and the corporate structuring typical of this highly regulated sector. The firm can be reached directly on WhatsApp for a free consultation.

A sector shaped by strategic national importance

This sector's legal work is shaped by its close connection to government contracts and specific regulatory oversight given its strategic nature, meaning businesses here operate under considerably more scrutiny and procedural formality than most commercial sectors.

This heightened scrutiny extends across the full relationship, from initial contractor qualification through ongoing performance and security compliance obligations that don't arise in typical commercial contracting.

Government defense contracts

This falls under our Government Contracts & Public Procurement service within Public & Regulatory, covering the specific tender and contracting process for defense-related procurement, which typically involves additional security clearance and confidentiality requirements beyond standard government contracting.

We help contractors understand these additional requirements early, since a security or confidentiality gap discovered mid-contract creates considerably more disruption than addressing it during initial qualification.

Aerospace-specific regulatory matters

This connects to our Aviation Legal Services within Sector-Specific Law, and aerospace businesses specifically — whether involved in manufacturing, maintenance, or related services — need to navigate GACA requirements alongside the defense-specific contracting rules that may apply to a given project.

This dual regulatory layer, aviation-specific and defense-specific, requires attorneys comfortable navigating both frameworks simultaneously rather than treating them as separate compliance exercises.

Corporate structuring for a highly regulated sector

Corporate structures in this sector often need to account for foreign ownership restrictions or security clearance requirements that don't apply to typical foreign investment, and our Corporate & Commercial practice structures these arrangements with genuine attention to these sector-specific constraints.

Getting this structure right at formation avoids complications later when a contract requires demonstrating compliance with ownership or control restrictions specific to defense-related work.

Joint ventures and technology partnerships

Defense and aerospace projects frequently involve joint ventures with international technical partners bringing specialized capability, and structuring these partnerships requires careful attention to technology transfer restrictions, offset obligations often tied to major defense contracts, and how control is actually allocated between parties.

We help Saudi entities and international partners structure these relationships with realistic expectations about the regulatory constraints specific to defense-related technology sharing.

Confidentiality and security compliance

Given the sensitive nature of much defense and aerospace work, confidentiality obligations and security compliance requirements run through contracts, employment relationships, and corporate governance in ways that don't arise in typical commercial sectors, and we help clients build these considerations into their standard operating agreements.

This isn't an afterthought layered onto standard commercial documentation — it needs to be built into agreements from the start given the genuine sensitivity involved.

Disputes in a highly regulated environment

Where a dispute arises in this sector — a contract performance disagreement, a joint venture conflict — resolving it requires attorneys who understand both the underlying commercial issue and the additional procedural and confidentiality constraints that shape how such disputes are actually handled.

We coordinate with our Disputes & Litigation practice where formal proceedings become necessary, ensuring these sector-specific constraints are respected throughout.
