Advertising & Marketing — Sector Legal Services

Advertising and marketing businesses need counsel on trademark and copyright protection for campaigns and creative assets, and consumer protection compliance around advertising claims. Reach out on WhatsApp to talk through your options.

A sector where creative value meets compliance obligations

This sector's legal needs center on protecting creative and brand assets while ensuring advertising claims comply with consumer protection standards, meaning an advertising agency or marketing business genuinely needs both dimensions handled well simultaneously.

We support both agencies creating campaigns and the brands commissioning them, understanding that these two client types have genuinely different but related legal needs.

Protecting creative campaign assets

This falls under our Copyright and Trademark Registration services within IP, and creative campaigns involve genuine intellectual property — copy, imagery, campaign concepts, taglines — that deserve protection commensurate with the investment made in developing them.

We help clients understand which elements of a campaign are protectable and how, since not every creative element receives the same type or level of protection.

Consumer protection compliance for advertising claims

This connects to our Consumer Protection service within Consumer & E-commerce, and advertising claims face genuine regulatory scrutiny around accuracy and substantiation, particularly for health, financial, or performance claims that carry real consumer reliance implications.

We help clients review campaign claims before launch, since discovering a compliance issue after significant media spend is a considerably worse position than confirming claims are substantiated beforehand.

Agency-client contract structuring

Agreements between agencies and clients involve their own specific considerations around ownership of creative work product, usage rights, exclusivity, and compensation structures, and we help both agencies and clients structure these relationships to clearly define expectations from the outset.

Getting ownership questions right at the contract stage avoids disputes that commonly arise when a campaign succeeds and questions emerge about who actually owns the resulting creative assets.

Influencer and endorsement marketing considerations

As influencer marketing has grown substantially, this raises specific questions around disclosure requirements, endorsement authenticity standards, and how liability is allocated between a brand and an influencer if a marketing claim proves problematic.

We help brands and agencies structure influencer agreements that address these considerations clearly, reducing exposure if a campaign attracts regulatory or consumer scrutiny.

Comparative and competitive advertising

Advertising that references or compares against competitors carries specific legal considerations around accuracy, fairness, and potential trademark or unfair competition concerns, and we help clients understand where the line sits between legitimate competitive advertising and content that creates genuine legal exposure.

This guidance matters most before a campaign launches, not after a competitor raises an objection.

Disputes in advertising and marketing relationships

Where a dispute arises — a creative ownership disagreement, a consumer protection complaint, a competitor challenge — we handle these with genuine understanding of how the advertising industry actually operates, coordinating with our Disputes & Litigation practice where formal proceedings become necessary.

This sector-specific understanding meaningfully strengthens how these disputes are actually resolved.
