Real Estate — Sector Legal Services

Real estate businesses in Riyadh — developers, investors, and property managers — rely on this firm for everything from title deed matters to off-plan sales compliance under the Wafi system. Message the firm on WhatsApp to discuss your property matter.

A market spanning individual transactions to major developments

Riyadh's real estate market spans everything from individual property transactions to large-scale development projects, each with its own compliance and dispute considerations, and businesses operating across this range need a firm comfortable moving between both scales.

This breadth means the legal work here genuinely varies by client type — a developer managing hundreds of off-plan units faces different obligations than an investor holding a handful of income properties, even though both ultimately rely on the same underlying property law framework.

Title, registration, and the foundation of every transaction

Every property transaction ultimately depends on clean, properly registered title, and our Real Estate & Property Law practice handles the verification work that confirms a seller genuinely owns what they're selling, free of undisclosed liens or competing claims.

This foundational work matters as much for a routine purchase as for a complex commercial development, since a title problem discovered after closing is considerably more expensive to resolve than one caught during due diligence.

Off-plan sales and Wafi compliance

Developers selling units before construction completes operate under the Wafi system's specific escrow and registration requirements, designed to protect buyers from exactly the kind of project failure that used to leave off-plan purchasers with no recourse.

We advise developers on structuring compliant off-plan sales from the outset, and separately support buyers verifying that a specific project's Wafi registration is genuine and current before committing significant funds.

When ownership itself is disputed

Title and land disputes arise from competing ownership claims, boundary disagreements, or registry inaccuracies, and these cases often require reconstructing a property's ownership history through multiple prior transactions to establish who actually holds valid title today.

These disputes can take real time to resolve properly, which is exactly why we recommend thorough title verification before a purchase rather than discovering a competing claim only after a transaction has already closed.

Serving both sides of the property relationship

Developers rely on us for project structuring, off-plan compliance, and defending against buyer disputes, while individual investors and buyers rely on the same underlying expertise to verify a purchase before committing and to pursue a remedy if something goes wrong afterward.

Where a matter involves fraud specifically — misrepresentation or a deliberately fraudulent sale — this connects to our Consumer & E-commerce practice's real estate fraud service, working alongside the property team rather than as a separate disconnected matter.

Reaching the firm about a property matter

We can typically give an initial assessment of what's involved and a realistic path forward within the same conversation.

Why early documentation prevents later disputes

Many of the property disputes we handle could have been avoided if the original agreement had been documented more clearly from the start — who was responsible for which obligation, what timeline was actually agreed, and what happens precisely if circumstances change.

We help clients document these details clearly from the outset, whether they're developers drafting a sale agreement or investors negotiating purchase terms, since early clarity significantly reduces the likelihood of a costly dispute later.

Do you represent both developers and individual buyers?

Yes — our Real Estate & Construction practice covers developer-side work, while individual buyer disputes fall under our Consumer & E-commerce practice's real estate fraud service.

Can you help with Wafi-registered off-plan sales?

Yes — off-plan sales compliance under the Wafi system is a core part of our Real Estate & Construction practice.

How do I verify a title is genuinely clean before purchasing?

We conduct title verification checking for liens, competing claims, and registry accuracy before you commit to a purchase — this is standard due diligence we recommend for any significant transaction.

What protections does Wafi registration actually provide buyers?

Wafi requires developers to hold buyer funds in escrow tied to construction progress, meaning a failed project doesn't simply leave buyers having paid for units that were never built.

How long do title disputes typically take to resolve?

It varies considerably depending on how far back the ownership history needs to be reconstructed, which is exactly why thorough verification before a purchase is so much more efficient than resolving a dispute afterward.
