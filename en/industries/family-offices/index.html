Family Offices — Sector Legal Services

Family offices rely on this firm's Private Client practice for family business succession planning, inheritance and estate structuring, and coordinating with corporate structuring where the family's wealth is tied to an operating business. Get in touch on WhatsApp to see how the firm can help.

Managing some of the most personal legal relationships

Family offices manage some of the most personal and long-term legal relationships this firm handles, spanning succession, inheritance, and often an underlying family business, meaning these relationships genuinely differ from a typical corporate client in both duration and the personal stakes involved.

We approach family office relationships with this long-term perspective in mind, building genuine familiarity with a family's specific situation over years rather than treating each matter as a standalone transaction.

Family business succession alongside personal estate planning

Our Private Client practice coordinates family business succession with personal estate and inheritance matters as one picture, recognizing that a family's wealth planning and their business's operational continuity are genuinely interconnected rather than separate concerns.

We help families navigate the sometimes difficult conversations around succession timing and structure, since these decisions carry both financial and genuine family relationship implications.

Inheritance and estate structuring

This involves understanding how fixed Sharia inheritance shares apply to a family's specific situation, and how tools like wills and waqf endowments can provide additional flexibility within that framework for families with more complex wealth structures or specific charitable intentions.

We help families understand these tools well before they become urgently needed, since proactive planning consistently produces more options than reactive planning under pressure.

Coordinating with corporate structuring

Where a family's wealth is tied to an operating business, we coordinate closely with our Corporate & Commercial practice, since decisions about business ownership structure, governance, and eventual transfer have real implications for the family's broader wealth planning.

This integrated approach means a family doesn't need to separately engage different specialists for what is, in practice, one interconnected wealth and succession picture.

Multi-generational wealth planning

Family offices increasingly think in terms of multi-generational planning — structures that account not just for the current generation's needs but for how wealth and business ownership will actually work across future generations with potentially different interests and involvement levels.

We help families think through these longer-term structural questions, since a structure that works well for one generation doesn't always translate cleanly to the next.

International considerations for globally-connected families

Many family offices manage wealth and family members spanning multiple countries, raising questions about how Saudi inheritance and estate frameworks interact with foreign jurisdictions where family members or assets are located.

We help families understand this interaction, coordinating with foreign counsel where necessary rather than assuming a purely domestic framework addresses every consideration.

Complete confidentiality throughout the relationship

Private Client relationships are built on long-term trust and full confidentiality, and we understand that family office matters often touch sensitive family dynamics as much as legal structure, requiring discretion beyond standard attorney-client confidentiality expectations.

This confidentiality extends across every dimension of the relationship, from routine estate planning conversations to more sensitive succession or family dynamic discussions.
