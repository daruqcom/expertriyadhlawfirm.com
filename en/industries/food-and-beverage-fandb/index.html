Food & Beverage (F&B) — Sector Legal Services

Food and beverage businesses need counsel on consumer protection compliance, municipal licensing and violations, and franchise structuring for restaurant and F&B brands. Reach the firm on WhatsApp to scope out your options.

A sector combining health regulation and consumer trust

F&B businesses navigate municipal health and licensing requirements alongside consumer protection obligations and, for growing brands, franchise structuring, meaning a restaurant or F&B brand genuinely needs several distinct legal dimensions handled well simultaneously.

We support this full range, from single-location restaurants managing routine compliance to growing brands structuring their first franchise agreements.

Municipal licensing for restaurants

This falls under our Municipal Violations service within Public & Regulatory, which also covers licensing compliance, and restaurant licensing involves health and safety requirements specific to food service that differ meaningfully from general commercial licensing.

We help F&B businesses understand these specific requirements before opening, since discovering a compliance gap after significant buildout investment is a considerably worse position than confirming requirements during initial planning.

Consumer protection compliance

Our Consumer & E-commerce practice covers this directly, and F&B businesses face specific consumer protection considerations around food labeling, allergen disclosure, and advertising claims that carry genuine regulatory scrutiny given the health implications involved.

We help F&B businesses build compliance practices that genuinely protect both consumers and the business from the disputes that arise when these standards aren't met.

Franchise structuring for growing F&B brands

This connects to our Franchise service within Corporate & Commercial, and F&B brands considering franchising need agreements that protect brand consistency and quality standards while giving franchisees genuine operational flexibility and a fair path to profitability.

We help brands structure these agreements to avoid the common tension between over-controlling franchise terms that discourage franchisee investment and under-protective terms that risk brand dilution.

Supply chain and vendor agreements

F&B businesses rely on contracts with food suppliers, equipment vendors, and delivery partners, each needing attention to quality specifications, delivery reliability, and liability allocation if a supply issue affects food safety or service quality.

We draft these contracts with genuine understanding of F&B operational realities, not generic commercial templates.

International and multi-location brand considerations

As Saudi Arabia's F&B sector attracts international brands and local brands expand across multiple locations, structuring these relationships requires attention to brand licensing terms, quality control across locations, and how liability is allocated when problems arise at a specific location versus the broader brand.

We help both international entrants and expanding local brands navigate these multi-location considerations.

Disputes in F&B business relationships

Where a dispute arises — a franchise disagreement, a supplier quality issue, a consumer complaint that's escalated — we handle these with genuine understanding of F&B operations, coordinating with our Disputes & Litigation practice where formal proceedings become necessary.

This sector-specific understanding meaningfully strengthens how these disputes are actually resolved.
