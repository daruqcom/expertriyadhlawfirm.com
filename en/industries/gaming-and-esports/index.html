Gaming & Esports — Sector Legal Services

Gaming and esports businesses need counsel on player and team contracts, event licensing, and protecting game and brand IP through copyright and trademark registration. Get in touch on WhatsApp — the firm typically responds the same day.

A fast-growing sector still finding its regulatory footing

Saudi Arabia's significant investment in esports has created a fast-growing sector with legal needs spanning contracts, IP protection, and event regulatory matters, reflecting the Kingdom's deliberate strategy of positioning itself as a genuine global esports hub.

This rapid growth means the sector's legal framework continues developing alongside it, requiring attorneys who track this specific area closely rather than applying outdated assumptions from traditional sports or general commercial law.

Esports team and player contracts

This falls under our Sports & Esports Law service within Sector-Specific Law, covering player agreements, team employment structures, and the performance and conduct provisions specific to competitive gaming that a generic employment contract often fails to address.

We help teams and organizations structure these agreements with genuine attention to esports-specific practices, including streaming rights and sponsorship obligations that don't arise in traditional sports contracts.

Protecting game and brand intellectual property

This connects to our Copyright service within IP, and game developers and esports organizations need to protect both the underlying creative work — game code, art, narrative content — and the brand identity built around teams and events.

We help clients understand which IP protections actually apply to their specific situation, since game-related IP questions can be genuinely more complex than standard copyright or trademark matters.

Event licensing for tournaments and competitions

Major esports tournaments require licensing and regulatory coordination similar to other major events, and we help organizers navigate this process, particularly as the scale of Saudi esports events has grown considerably in recent years.

We stay current on how this specific licensing landscape is actually being applied, since requirements have evolved alongside the sector's rapid expansion.

Streaming and content monetization rights

Esports revenue increasingly depends on streaming and content rights arrangements, and structuring these agreements requires attention to platform-specific terms, revenue sharing between players, teams, and organizers, and how content ownership actually works when multiple parties contribute to a broadcast.

We help clients structure these arrangements with genuine understanding of how streaming platforms and content monetization actually work in practice.

Sponsorship and brand partnership structuring

Esports organizations increasingly rely on sponsorship revenue, and structuring these partnerships clearly — what a sponsor's investment actually entitles them to, exclusivity terms, performance expectations — helps avoid disputes once a sponsorship relationship is underway.

We help both esports organizations and sponsors structure these relationships with realistic expectations built in from the start.

Disputes in gaming and esports relationships

Where a dispute arises — a player contract disagreement, an IP infringement question, a sponsorship conflict — we handle these with genuine sector-specific understanding, since gaming and esports disputes often turn on industry practices a generalist approach might miss.

This specific knowledge meaningfully strengthens how these disputes are actually resolved.
