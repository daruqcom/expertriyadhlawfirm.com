About Us

A licensed, full-service law firm built for Riyadh's business community — from first-time foreign investors to established companies managing an active dispute.

Expert Riyadh Law Firm is a licensed law firm based in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, providing full-service legal representation across corporate, regulatory, dispute, and personal-status matters. The firm is built for anyone who needs a licensed Saudi lawyer — company founders, boards, individuals, and government-adjacent entities. Message the firm on WhatsApp to discuss how the team can help.

Built around Riyadh's role as the capital

Riyadh is the seat of government and the corporate and financial capital of Saudi Arabia, and the firm's practice reflects that: corporate formation, MISA foreign investment licensing, capital markets, and regulatory work sit alongside full coverage of litigation, family law, and every other practice area a Riyadh business or individual needs.

Licensed, bilingual, and built for the full lifecycle of a matter

Attorneys at the firm work in both Arabic and English, and matters are handled from the first consultation through filing, court representation, and, where a case is won, enforcement. The firm holds current registration with the Saudi Ministry of Justice.

Clients range from foreign investors licensing their first Saudi entity, to shareholders in an active dispute, to individuals navigating a personal-status matter — each handled with the same standard of care regardless of the size of the matter.
