Common e-commerce disputes

This covers a range of online transaction issues — goods that were never delivered, products that didn't match their description, and disagreements over return or refund policies, along with platform compliance questions specific to online sellers. Where the seller's conduct crosses from a commercial dispute into deliberate deception, it is handled alongside our fraud & scam practice.

The E-commerce Law and MAROOF registration requirements set out specific obligations for online sellers, giving consumers a clearer basis to pursue a complaint when those obligations aren't met.

Resolving an e-commerce dispute

For consumers, this involves documenting the transaction and any communication with the seller, then pursuing resolution through the appropriate channel, up to formal legal action where needed. Offline retail complaints follow the broader consumer protection track instead.

For sellers and platforms, this practice advises on E-commerce Law compliance and MAROOF registration to minimize disputes in the first place, and represents businesses in responding to a consumer complaint or regulatory inquiry.

Why acting within the return window matters more than it seems

Return and refund rights for online purchases are often subject to specific time windows, and letting time pass while deciding whether an issue is worth pursuing can mean the window closes before a decision is actually made, turning a straightforward return into a considerably harder dispute over whether any remedy remains available at all.

We recommend acting promptly on any online purchase issue rather than deliberating for an extended period, since raising the issue and preserving your options costs little, while a closed window can eliminate a straightforward remedy that was easily available just days earlier. If the product itself carried a flaw that only appeared after the return window, a hidden defect claim may still be available.

Why checking MAROOF registration before purchasing offers real protection

MAROOF registration signals that an online seller has taken steps to demonstrate compliance with the E-commerce Law, and checking this status before making a purchase from an unfamiliar seller can be a genuinely useful early warning sign, since a seller that hasn't bothered with this basic compliance step may also be more likely to fall short on other consumer protection obligations that matter if something goes wrong.

We recommend this quick check as a simple precaution before purchasing from a seller you don't already know and trust, since the few moments it takes can meaningfully reduce the risk of ending up in a dispute with a seller who was never genuinely committed to standard consumer protections in the first place.

Direct Answers

What should I do if an online purchase never arrived?

Document the order, payment, and any communication with the seller, and contact us to assess the right path to resolution, including escalation through the appropriate channel if the seller isn't responsive.

What obligations does MAROOF registration create for a seller?

It's designed to demonstrate compliance with the E-commerce Law, including obligations around business transparency and consumer protection. We can confirm the specific requirements for a seller's situation.

Can this practice help a platform respond to a consumer complaint?

Yes — we represent online sellers and platforms in responding to consumer complaints and any related regulatory inquiries.

Does it matter how quickly I act if a product doesn't match its description?

Yes, significantly — return and refund windows are often time-limited, so acting promptly rather than deliberating for weeks helps preserve a straightforward remedy before the window closes.

Should I check a seller's MAROOF registration before buying from them?

It's a worthwhile precaution, especially with an unfamiliar seller — registration signals a baseline compliance effort, and its absence can be an early warning sign worth taking seriously.

Is it too late to pursue a return if I've already waited a few weeks?

It depends on the specific return window and circumstances — reach out regardless, since we can assess whether any remedy is still realistically available in your specific situation.
