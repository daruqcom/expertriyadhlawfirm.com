Lawyer in Al Olaya

Al Olaya is Riyadh's central business and financial district, and the lawyers serve the corporate clients, family offices, and financial institutions concentrated there with a focus on corporate, finance, and dispute matters. Contact us on WhatsApp.

This page is part of an independent lawyer directory. It gives general information on legal services in Saudi Arabia, which is not legal advice, and the directory is not a law firm.

The heart of Riyadh's institutional business activity

Home to the Kingdom Centre, Al Faisaliah Tower, and a concentration of corporate headquarters along King Fahd Road, Al Olaya is where much of Riyadh's institutional business activity is based, and the lawyers' core Corporate & Commercial and Finance, Banking & Tax practices see particularly heavy demand from clients here.

This concentration of head offices, banks, and financial institutions means the legal work coming out of Al Olaya skews heavily toward corporate governance, financing arrangements, and commercial contracts, matters where getting the underlying documentation right the first time matters, given the scale typically involved.

Corporate formation, governance, and financing at scale

Companies headquartered in Al Olaya frequently need support with everything from initial formation and MISA foreign investment licensing through to ongoing governance obligations, board resolutions, shareholder agreements, and the kind of documentation that becomes critical the moment a new investor requests due diligence or a dispute arises between partners.

The district's concentration of banks and finance companies also means a steady flow of financing-related work: reviewing facility agreements before signing, structuring Sharia-compliant financing, and advising on the SAMA regulatory requirements that govern lending relationships in Saudi Arabia.

Family offices and private wealth alongside corporate work

Beyond publicly-facing corporate headquarters, Al Olaya is also home to a meaningful concentration of family offices managing private wealth across generations, a client base with different needs from a typical corporate client, often touching succession planning, private trust-like structures, and coordinating family business ownership with personal estate planning.

This dual character, large corporate entities alongside private family wealth, is part of why the lawyers' Private Client practice works so closely with Corporate & Commercial for clients based in this district specifically.

When a commercial relationship in Al Olaya becomes a dispute

Given the volume and value of commercial activity concentrated here, disputes are inevitable, a disagreement over contract terms, a shareholder dispute at a company headquartered nearby, or a financing arrangement that's gone wrong. The lawyers' Disputes & Litigation practice handles these matters through the commercial courts or arbitration, often for the same corporate clients the lawyers already represent on the transactional side.

Having the same firm handle both the underlying corporate relationship and any dispute that later arises means the attorneys involved already understand the commercial context, rather than starting from zero with an unfamiliar new firm.

Working with clients based in Al Olaya

Most matters for clients in this district are handled remotely, reviewing a contract, drafting a board resolution, or advising on a financing structure rarely requires an in-person meeting, and WhatsApp remains the fastest way to reach an attorney for an initial conversation about a new matter.

Where an in-person meeting adds value, given Al Olaya's central location this is often one of the more convenient districts in the city to arrange one, and the lawyers accommodate this readily by appointment.
